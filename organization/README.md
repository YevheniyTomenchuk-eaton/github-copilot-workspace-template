## 🔗 How Other Sections Reuse This

The organization is the **single source of truth** for who is on the project.
Other sections link to it rather than duplicating the data:

- The [Email toolkit](../toolkit/email/README.md) can resolve a recipient's
  display name and address from a person page instead of hardcoding it.
- An agenda or meeting note can link presenters and attendees to their person pages.
- Ceremony pages link their participants to [teams](foundation/teams/README.md) and people.

When a person's details change, you update one page and every link stays correct.
